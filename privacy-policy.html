Privacy Policy for ConnectMe

Last updated: June 14, 2026

ConnectMe ("we", "our", "the app") is a chat application that allows users to create profiles, communicate with other users, and share text, images, and voice messages. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

When you use ConnectMe, we may collect the following information:

  • Account information: username, password (stored securely), email address (if you sign in with Google), gender, age, and profile photo.
  • Profile content: "About" text, profile picture, and any other information you choose to add to your profile.
  • Messages and media: text messages, images, and voice messages you send or receive within the app.
  • Usage and device data: online/offline status, last seen timestamp, push notification token (FCM token), and basic device information needed to deliver notifications.
  • Camera, microphone, and storage access: used only when you choose to take a photo, record a voice message, or select an image to send.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To enable chat functionality between users.
  • To show your profile to other users (based on your privacy settings).
  • To send push notifications about new messages.
  • To enforce our community guidelines, including reviewing reports of abuse and taking action such as warnings or account bans.

3. Data Storage

All data is stored using Google Firebase (Realtime Database, Authentication, and Cloud Messaging) and Cloudinary (for image and audio file hosting). Data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Information is shared only with:

  • Other users you choose to chat with (your profile name, photo, and messages).
  • Service providers that help us run the app (Firebase, Cloudinary), strictly for the purpose of providing app functionality.

5. Reporting and Safety

ConnectMe includes tools to block and report other users. Reports are reviewed, and accounts that receive multiple reports may be automatically restricted or banned. If you encounter content involving child sexual abuse or exploitation, please report it immediately using the in-app "Report" feature and also contact us using the email below — such content is never tolerated and will result in immediate account removal and reporting to relevant authorities.

6. Your Choices and Rights

  • You can edit or update your profile information at any time from the Settings screen.
  • You can control who can see your last seen, profile photo, and "About" info from Privacy Settings.
  • You can permanently delete your account and all associated data from Settings → Delete Account. This removes your profile, chats, and messages from our systems.
  • If you are unable to access the app, you may request account deletion by emailing us at the address below.

7. Children's Privacy

ConnectMe is intended for users who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a user under 18 has created an account, we will take steps to remove that account and associated data.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
